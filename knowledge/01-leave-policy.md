# Nimbus Dynamics — Leave Policy (NDPL-HR-001)

**Document ID:** NDPL-HR-001 · **Version:** 4.2 · **Effective:** 1 January 2026 · **Owner:** People Operations · **Applies to:** All permanent employees of Nimbus Dynamics Private Limited in India (Hyderabad, Mumbai, Bengaluru)

> In case of any conflict between this policy and any FAQ, email, portal help text or verbal communication, **this policy document prevails**.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy defines the categories of leave available to employees, the entitlement for each category, how leave accrues, how it is applied for and approved, and what happens to unused leave. It applies to all permanent employees. Employees on probation are covered by Section 9 and by the Probation and Confirmation Policy (NDPL-HR-003). Contractors, consultants and interns are covered by NDPL-HR-009 and are **not** eligible for the paid leave categories in this document unless explicitly stated.

The leave year at Nimbus Dynamics is the **calendar year (1 January – 31 December)**. The working week is **Monday to Friday**. Saturdays and Sundays are non-working days for all locations.

## 2. Leave categories at a glance

| Casual Leave | CL | 12 days | Credited in full on 1 January | **No** — lapses on 31 December | No |
| Sick Leave | SL | 10 days | Credited in full on 1 January | Yes, up to a cumulative cap of 30 days | No |

| Marriage Leave | — | 5 working days, once during employment | — | — | — |

All leave balances are maintained in **days**. A half day is recorded as 0.5.

## 3. Casual Leave (CL)

3.1 Every permanent employee receives **12 days of Casual Leave** on 1 January each year. Employees who join mid-year receive a pro-rated credit of **1 day for every completed month** remaining in the year, calculated from the date of joining.

3.2 Casual Leave is intended for short, unplanned personal needs. A maximum of **3 consecutive working days** of CL may be taken at a time. Requests for more than 3 consecutive days must be applied as Privilege Leave.

3.3 CL requires a notice of **at least 2 working days**. In a genuine emergency, the employee must inform their reporting manager by phone or message on the same day and regularise the leave in the HR portal within 2 working days of returning.

3.4 CL **cannot be clubbed** (combined) with Privilege Leave in the same continuous stretch. CL may be combined with a weekend or a public holiday, subject to the sandwich rule in Section 8.

3.5 CL may be taken as a **half day** (first half or second half). A half day is deducted as 0.5 days.

3.6 **Casual Leave does not carry forward.** Any unused CL balance lapses on 31 December and is not encashable. (See Section 12 for superseded rules.)

3.7 Unused CL cannot be converted to any other leave type.

## 4. Sick Leave (SL)

4.1 Every permanent employee receives **10 days of Sick Leave** on 1 January each year. Mid-year joiners receive a pro-rated credit of **1 day for every completed month** remaining in the year.

4.2 SL is for the employee's own illness or injury, or for a medical appointment that cannot be scheduled outside working hours.

4.3 Sick Leave of **more than 2 consecutive working days** requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner. The certificate must be uploaded to the HR portal within **3 working days of rejoining**. If the certificate is not submitted, the days beyond the second day are converted to Loss of Pay.

4.4 SL may be applied **retroactively** — an employee who was unwell may apply for the days already taken within 3 working days of returning to work. SL for a **future date** is permitted only for a planned medical procedure or appointment and requires a doctor's note at the time of application.

4.5 Unused SL **carries forward** to the next year. The accumulated SL balance is capped at **30 days**; any excess lapses on 31 December.

4.6 SL is not encashable at any time, including at separation.

4.7 The sandwich rule (Section 8) **does not apply** to Sick Leave.

## 5. Privilege Leave (PL)

5.1 Privilege Leave (also referred to as Earned Leave or Annual Leave in older documents) is credited at **1.5 days for every completed month of service**, giving 18 days in a full year. Accrual is credited on the last day of each month.

5.2 PL is intended for planned time off such as vacations, family events and travel.

5.3 **Notice requirements for PL:**
- Up to 3 working days of PL: apply at least **7 calendar days** in advance.
- More than 3 working days of PL: apply at least **15 calendar days** in advance.
- Requests that do not meet the notice period may be approved at the manager's discretion but are not guaranteed.

5.4 The minimum PL that can be applied is **1 full day**. PL cannot be taken as a half day.

5.5 PL may be combined with weekends and public holidays. The sandwich rule in Section 8 applies.

5.6 **Carry forward and lapse:** Unused PL carries forward to the next year up to a maximum accumulated balance of **30 days**. Any PL in excess of 30 days on 31 December lapses, unless it has been encashed under Section 5.7.

5.7 **Encashment:** Employees may encash up to **10 days of PL** once per year during the December encashment window (1–15 December), provided a minimum balance of 10 days is retained after encashment. Encashment is calculated on basic salary only.

5.8 PL is paid out in full at separation as per NDPL-HR-008.

5.9 PL accrued during the probation period is credited to the employee's balance but **cannot be availed until confirmation** (see Section 9).

## 6. Loss of Pay (LOP)

6.1 When an employee has exhausted the relevant leave balance, additional absence is treated as **Loss of Pay**. LOP days are deducted from the monthly salary on a pro-rata basis.

6.2 LOP must be approved by the reporting manager **and** the HR Business Partner. LOP cannot be applied through the self-service HR Assistant; the employee must raise a request with their HRBP.

6.3 More than 10 days of LOP in a calendar year affects the employee's eligibility for the annual performance bonus, as described in the Compensation Policy.

6.4 Unauthorised absence (absence without an approved leave request) is treated as LOP and may attract disciplinary action under the Code of Conduct.

## 7. Applying for leave and approvals

7.1 All leave must be applied through the Nimbus HR Portal or through the HR Assistant, which submits the request to the portal on the employee's behalf.

7.2 Every leave request is routed to the employee's **reporting manager** for approval. A request stays in `PENDING_APPROVAL` status until the manager acts on it. Leave should not be treated as granted until it is `APPROVED`.

7.3 If a request remains pending for more than **3 working days**, it is automatically escalated to the skip-level manager.

7.4 An employee may not have two leave requests that overlap on any date. To change a request, cancel the existing one first.

7.5 **Cancellation and modification** of a leave request must be done in the HR Portal by the employee. The HR Assistant cannot cancel or modify a request. Approved leave that is cancelled before the start date is fully re-credited.

7.6 Leave cannot be applied for a date in the past, except Sick Leave under Section 4.4 and emergency Casual Leave under Section 3.3.

7.7 Leave cannot be applied on a Saturday, Sunday, a declared public holiday for the employee's location, or a shutdown day, because these are already non-working days. Employees should check the Holiday Calendar (NDPL-HR-002) for their location before applying.

7.8 Employees on **notice period** are subject to the leave restrictions in NDPL-HR-008 (Exit and Notice Period Policy).

7.9 Employees on maternity, paternity or adoption leave cannot apply for any other category of leave for the duration of that leave (see NDPL-HR-006).

## 8. Sandwich rule

8.1 If an employee takes leave on the working day **immediately before** and the working day **immediately after** a weekend or public holiday, the intervening weekend / holiday days are **also counted as leave** and deducted from the same leave balance.

8.2 Example: Leave on Friday 25 September and Monday 28 September deducts **4 days** (Friday, Saturday, Sunday, Monday), not 2.

8.3 The sandwich rule applies to **Casual Leave and Privilege Leave**. It does **not** apply to Sick Leave, Compensatory Off, Bereavement Leave or Maternity/Paternity leave.

8.4 The rule does not apply if leave is taken on only one side of the weekend or holiday.

## 9. Leave during probation

9.1 Employees on probation are eligible for Casual Leave and Sick Leave on a pro-rated basis (1 day per completed month for each), as described in NDPL-HR-003.

9.2 Privilege Leave **accrues** during probation but **cannot be availed** until the employee is confirmed. An employee on probation who needs planned time off beyond their CL balance must apply for LOP through their HRBP.

9.3 Leave taken during probation may extend the probation period at the discretion of the reporting manager if the total leave exceeds 5 working days.

## 10. Bereavement and marriage leave

10.1 **Bereavement Leave** of up to 5 working days is granted on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, parent-in-law, sibling). It must be applied within 15 days of the event and is not deducted from any other balance.

10.2 **Marriage Leave** of 5 consecutive working days is granted once during employment for the employee's own marriage, with at least 30 days' notice.

10.3 These categories are administered manually by People Operations and are **not available through the HR Assistant** or the self-service portal. Employees should email people-ops@nimbusdyn.example.

## 11. Year-end shutdown

11.1 Nimbus Dynamics observes an annual **year-end shutdown from 28 December to 31 December**. All offices are closed.

11.2 Shutdown days that fall on working days are **automatically deducted from the employee's Privilege Leave balance** in the December payroll run. Employees are advised to retain at least 4 days of PL for this purpose.

11.3 If an employee's PL balance is insufficient to cover the shutdown, the shortfall is treated as Loss of Pay. Casual Leave is **not** used for the shutdown.

11.4 Employees required to work during the shutdown (for example, on-call engineers) are exempt from the deduction and earn Compensatory Off as per NDPL-HR-007.

## 12. Superseded clauses (for reference only)

The following clauses were in force in earlier versions and are **no longer applicable**. They are retained here so that employees reading older communications are not misled.

- **Version 3.x (2023–2024):** Casual Leave carry forward of up to 5 days was permitted. *Withdrawn with effect from 1 January 2025.* CL now lapses in full on 31 December.
- **Version 3.x (2023–2024):** Privilege Leave was called "Earned Leave (EL)" and accrued at 1.25 days per month (15 days per year). *Revised to 1.5 days per month (18 days per year) with effect from 1 January 2025.*
- **Version 4.0 (2025):** PL encashment was capped at 5 days. *Raised to 10 days with effect from 1 January 2026.*
- **Version 4.1 (2025):** The PL carry-forward cap was 45 days. *Reduced to 30 days with effect from 1 January 2026; balances above 30 days on 31 December 2025 were encashed in the January 2026 payroll.*

## 13. Definitions

- **Working day:** Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays declared for the employee's location and shutdown days.
- **Calendar day:** Any day, including weekends and holidays.
- **Reporting manager:** The person recorded as the employee's manager in the HR Portal.
- **HRBP:** HR Business Partner assigned to the employee's department.
- **Confirmation:** The date on which an employee's probation ends and permanent status is confirmed in writing.
