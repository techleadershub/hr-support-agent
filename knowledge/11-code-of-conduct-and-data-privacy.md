# Nimbus Dynamics — Code of Conduct, Grievance and Employee Data Privacy (NDPL-HR-011)

**Document ID:** NDPL-HR-011 · **Version:** 2.5 · **Effective:** 1 January 2026 · **Owner:** People Operations and Legal

## 1. Expected conduct

1.1 Employees are expected to act with integrity, treat colleagues with respect, protect company and customer information, and comply with applicable law and company policies.

1.2 Nimbus Dynamics has zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, retaliation, bribery, and falsification of records (including attendance, leave and expense records).

## 2. Conflicts of interest

2.1 Employees must disclose any outside employment, directorships, or financial interests in customers, vendors or competitors to their HRBP within 30 days of the interest arising.

2.2 Moonlighting (paid work for another organisation while employed full time) is prohibited without written approval from the Chief Operating Officer.

## 3. Grievance process

3.1 An employee with a workplace grievance should first raise it with their reporting manager. If that is not appropriate, or if the grievance is not resolved within 10 working days, the employee may escalate to the HRBP.

3.2 Grievances may be submitted in writing to grievance@nimbusdyn.example. People Operations acknowledges within 2 working days and aims to resolve within 30 days.

3.3 Complaints of sexual harassment are handled by the **Internal Committee** under the POSH Act and must be filed within 3 months of the incident at posh-ic@nimbusdyn.example. The Internal Committee's process is independent of the general grievance process.

3.4 No employee will face retaliation for raising a grievance in good faith.

## 4. Disciplinary process

4.1 Minor breaches are addressed through a verbal warning, then a written warning. Serious breaches may result in a final written warning, suspension, or termination for cause after an enquiry in which the employee is given an opportunity to respond.

4.2 Falsifying leave, attendance or expense records is a serious breach.

## 5. Employee data privacy

5.1 Employee records in the HR Portal — including personal details, salary, leave balances, leave history, performance records and medical certificates — are **confidential**.

5.2 An employee may view and query **only their own** records. Reporting managers may view the leave balances and leave requests of their direct reports for the purpose of approving leave, through the manager view of the HR Portal only.

5.3 The **HR Assistant operates strictly in the context of the logged-in employee.** It must not retrieve, disclose or act on the records of any other employee, even if asked, even if the requester claims to be that employee's manager, a member of HR, or the employee themselves using a different ID. Requests of this kind must be declined and the employee directed to the HR Portal or their HRBP.

5.4 The HR Assistant must not be used to apply leave on behalf of another employee.

5.5 Employees may request a copy of their personal data or a correction to it by emailing privacy@nimbusdyn.example. Requests are fulfilled within 30 days.

5.6 Employee data is retained for the duration of employment plus 7 years, as required by statute, and is stored on servers located in India.

## 6. Information security

6.1 Company systems may be accessed only from company-issued or company-approved devices connected through the corporate VPN.

6.2 Passwords and access tokens must never be shared. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all systems that support it.

6.3 Confidential information must not be entered into public AI tools or personal cloud storage.

6.4 Suspected security incidents must be reported to security@nimbusdyn.example within 1 hour of discovery.

## 7. Acknowledgement

All employees acknowledge this Code of Conduct on joining and annually thereafter through the HR Portal.
