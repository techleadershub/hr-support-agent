# Nimbus Dynamics — Compensatory Off Policy (NDPL-HR-007)

**Document ID:** NDPL-HR-007 · **Version:** 1.4 · **Effective:** 1 January 2026 · **Owner:** People Operations

## 1. What is Compensatory Off

1.1 Compensatory Off (Comp Off, code **COMP_OFF**) is a paid day off earned by an employee who is required to work on a **Saturday, Sunday, national holiday, regional holiday for their location, or shutdown day**.

1.2 Comp Off is an earned balance, not an annual entitlement. An employee who has not worked on a non-working day has a Comp Off balance of zero.

## 2. Earning Comp Off

2.1 Comp Off is earned only when the weekend or holiday work was **approved in advance** by the reporting manager in the HR Portal (a "Weekend Work Request").

2.2 **Full day** Comp Off is earned for **6 or more hours** of work on the non-working day. **Half day** Comp Off is earned for **4 to 6 hours**. Less than 4 hours does not earn Comp Off.

2.3 A maximum of **2 Comp Off days can be earned in a calendar month**.

2.4 Comp Off must be claimed in the HR Portal within **7 days** of the day worked, with the manager's confirmation. Claims made after 7 days are not credited.

2.5 On-call engineers who are called out on a weekend earn Comp Off based on the actual hours logged in the incident system, subject to 2.2 and 2.3.

## 3. Availing Comp Off

3.1 Comp Off must be availed within **60 days of the date it was earned**. Unused Comp Off **expires automatically** after 60 days and cannot be extended, carried forward or encashed. The expiry date of each Comp Off day is shown in the leave balance.

3.2 Comp Off is applied through the standard leave module (or the HR Assistant) with leave type **COMP_OFF**. The same notice requirement as Casual Leave applies: at least **2 working days** in advance.

3.3 Comp Off is taken as a **full day**. Half-day Comp Off is only possible when a half day was earned under 2.2.

3.4 Comp Off can be combined with weekends and holidays. The sandwich rule (NDPL-HR-001, Section 8) does **not** apply to Comp Off.

3.5 Comp Off cannot be clubbed with Privilege Leave in the same continuous stretch. It may be clubbed with Casual Leave.

3.6 Where an employee has both Comp Off and Casual Leave available, **Comp Off closest to expiry should be used first**. The HR Portal enforces this order for CL and Comp Off requests on the same date.

## 4. Eligibility

4.1 Permanent employees (including those on probation) are eligible.

4.2 Contractors, consultants and interns are **not** eligible for Comp Off. Any weekend work by contractors is governed by their contract.

4.3 Employees at Senior Manager level and above are not eligible for Comp Off, as weekend availability is part of their role expectation.

## 5. Separation

5.1 Unused Comp Off is forfeited on the last working day and is not paid out.
