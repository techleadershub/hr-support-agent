# Nimbus Dynamics — Work From Home Policy (NDPL-HR-005)

**Document ID:** NDPL-HR-005 · **Version:** 3.1 · **Effective:** 1 January 2026 · **Owner:** People Operations

## 1. Principle

1.1 Work From Home (WFH) is a **working arrangement, not leave**. An employee working from home is on duty, is expected to be reachable during core hours, and is counted as present. WFH days are **not deducted** from any leave balance.

1.2 WFH is a privilege extended to support flexibility, not an entitlement. It may be withdrawn for business reasons or for misuse.

## 2. Entitlement

2.1 Confirmed permanent employees may work from home for **up to 2 days per week**, subject to the 3-days-in-office requirement in NDPL-HR-004.

2.2 Employees on **probation** may work from home for **up to 1 day per week**.

2.3 Employees on **notice period** require written approval from their reporting manager for each WFH day.

2.4 Contractors and consultants follow the working arrangement stated in their engagement contract.

2.5 Interns are expected to work from the office on all working days unless the internship agreement states otherwise.

## 3. Extended WFH

3.1 WFH for **more than 2 consecutive weeks** (for example, to care for a family member, or to work from a hometown) requires approval from the reporting manager **and** the HRBP, and must be recorded as "Extended WFH" in the HR Portal.

3.2 Extended WFH cannot exceed **8 weeks** in a calendar year.

3.3 Extended WFH from **outside India** is not permitted without approval from the Chief Operating Officer because of tax, data protection and insurance implications.

## 4. Requesting WFH

4.1 Regular WFH days must be recorded in the HR Portal at least **1 working day** in advance. Same-day WFH is permitted in unforeseen circumstances with the manager's consent by message.

4.2 WFH is recorded through the **attendance module**, not the leave module. The HR Assistant does not apply WFH on the employee's behalf; the employee must record it in the portal.

## 5. Expectations while working from home

5.1 Employees must be available on the company messaging platform and by phone during core hours (10:30 AM – 4:30 PM IST).

5.2 Cameras must be on for scheduled team meetings unless the meeting organiser states otherwise.

5.3 Employees must use the company-issued laptop and connect through the corporate VPN. Personal devices may not be used to access company systems (see NDPL-HR-011, Section 6).

5.4 A WFH day on which the employee is unwell and cannot work must be converted to Sick Leave. It is a Code of Conduct violation to log WFH while not working.

## 6. Equipment and expenses

6.1 Nimbus Dynamics provides a laptop, headset and a one-time home office allowance of **₹10,000** on confirmation for a chair or desk, claimable through NDPL-HR-010.

6.2 Broadband reimbursement of up to **₹1,000 per month** is available to confirmed employees who work from home at least 1 day per week, claimed quarterly with the invoice attached.
