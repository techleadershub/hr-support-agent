# Nimbus Dynamics — Probation and Confirmation Policy (NDPL-HR-003)

**Document ID:** NDPL-HR-003 · **Version:** 3.0 · **Effective:** 1 January 2026 · **Owner:** People Operations

## 1. Probation period

1.1 All new permanent employees serve a probation period of **6 months** from the date of joining. The probation end date is recorded in the employee profile.

1.2 Probation may be **extended once by up to 3 months** if performance or conduct has not met expectations. The extension must be communicated in writing before the original probation end date.

1.3 Employees whose probation is extended remain subject to this policy until the new end date.

1.4 Employees rehired within 12 months of leaving Nimbus Dynamics are not required to serve probation again.

## 2. Confirmation

2.1 A confirmation review is held in the final month of probation. The reporting manager submits a recommendation to People Operations.

2.2 Confirmation is effective from the day after the probation end date, once the confirmation letter is issued. Until the letter is issued, the employee's status remains `probation` in the HR Portal.

2.3 If no decision is communicated within 15 days after the probation end date, the employee is deemed confirmed.

## 3. Leave entitlement during probation

3.1 **Casual Leave:** credited at **1 day per completed month** of service during the year of joining, up to a maximum of 6 days in the joining year. Example: an employee who joins on 1 July receives 1 day of CL on 31 July, another on 31 August, and so on.

3.2 **Sick Leave:** credited at **1 day per completed month** of service during the year of joining, up to a maximum of 5 days in the joining year.

3.3 **Privilege Leave:** PL **accrues** at 1.5 days per completed month from the date of joining and is shown in the employee's balance, but it **cannot be availed** during probation. Any PL request from an employee on probation is rejected. The accrued balance becomes available on confirmation.

3.4 **Compensatory Off:** employees on probation may earn and avail Comp Off as per NDPL-HR-007.

3.5 Leave in excess of the available CL/SL balance during probation is treated as Loss of Pay and requires HRBP approval.

3.6 If total leave taken during probation exceeds **5 working days**, the reporting manager may extend the probation period by the number of days of leave taken.

## 4. Notice period during probation

4.1 The notice period for an employee on probation is **15 days** for either party, compared with 60 days for confirmed employees (see NDPL-HR-008).

## 5. Interns

5.1 Interns are engaged for a fixed term and are not permanent employees. They do not serve probation and are not confirmed.

5.2 Interns receive **1 day of Casual Leave and 0.5 day of Sick Leave per completed month** of the internship. Interns do not accrue Privilege Leave and are not eligible for Compensatory Off, encashment or carry forward.

5.3 Interns observe all national and regional holidays and the year-end shutdown without any deduction.

## 6. Benefits during probation

6.1 Employees on probation are covered by group medical insurance from the date of joining.

6.2 Employees on probation are not eligible for the learning reimbursement under NDPL-HR-012 until confirmation.

6.3 Work-from-home under NDPL-HR-005 is limited to **1 day per week** during probation.
