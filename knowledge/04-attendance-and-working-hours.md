# Nimbus Dynamics — Attendance and Working Hours Policy (NDPL-HR-004)

**Document ID:** NDPL-HR-004 · **Version:** 2.3 · **Effective:** 1 January 2026 · **Owner:** People Operations

## 1. Working hours

1.1 The standard working week is **Monday to Friday, 9 hours per day including a 1-hour lunch break** (8 productive hours), totalling 40 productive hours per week.

1.2 **Core hours** are **10:30 AM to 4:30 PM IST**. All employees are expected to be available during core hours regardless of whether they are working from the office or from home. Start and end times are flexible outside core hours, provided the 9-hour day is completed.

1.3 Teams supporting customers in other time zones may follow a shifted schedule agreed with their manager and recorded in the HR Portal. Shifted schedules must still include at least 4 hours of overlap with core hours.

## 2. Attendance recording

2.1 Attendance is recorded automatically through the office access card system when in office, and through the HR Portal check-in when working from home.

2.2 A day is counted as **present** if the recorded working time is at least 8 hours. A recorded working time of **4 to 8 hours** is counted as a **half day**. Less than 4 hours is counted as **absent** unless leave has been applied.

2.3 Attendance regularisation requests (for example, a forgotten check-in) must be raised in the HR Portal within **5 working days** and approved by the reporting manager. A maximum of 3 regularisations per month is allowed.

## 3. Late arrival

3.1 Arriving after 10:30 AM without prior intimation is recorded as a **late mark**.

3.2 **Three late marks in a calendar month** are converted to a half day of Casual Leave. If no CL balance is available, the half day is treated as Loss of Pay.

## 4. Half days

4.1 A half day of leave covers either the **first half (up to 1:30 PM)** or the **second half (from 1:30 PM)**.

4.2 Half days can be applied against Casual Leave and Sick Leave. Privilege Leave and Compensatory Off must be taken as full days.

## 5. Hybrid working

5.1 Permanent employees are expected to work from the office **at least 3 days per week**. The remaining days may be worked from home under NDPL-HR-005.

5.2 Team "anchor days" (days when the whole team is in office) are set by the reporting manager. Anchor days are mandatory office days.

## 6. Overtime and weekend work

6.1 Nimbus Dynamics does not pay overtime to salaried employees. Employees who are required by their manager to work on a weekend or a holiday earn **Compensatory Off** as per NDPL-HR-007.

6.2 Weekend work must be **approved in advance** by the reporting manager for Comp Off to be earned. Voluntary weekend work is not eligible.

## 7. Unauthorised absence

7.1 Absence without an approved leave request or an approved regularisation is **unauthorised absence**, recorded as Loss of Pay and reported to the HRBP.

7.2 Unauthorised absence of **3 or more consecutive working days** without any communication is treated as abandonment of employment and triggers the process in the Code of Conduct (NDPL-HR-011).
