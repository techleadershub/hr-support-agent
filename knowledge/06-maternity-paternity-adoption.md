# Nimbus Dynamics — Maternity, Paternity and Adoption Leave Policy (NDPL-HR-006)

**Document ID:** NDPL-HR-006 · **Version:** 2.0 · **Effective:** 1 January 2026 · **Owner:** People Operations

## 1. Maternity leave

1.1 Women employees who have worked for at least **80 days in the 12 months** preceding the expected date of delivery are entitled to **26 weeks** of paid maternity leave for the first two children, and **12 weeks** for the third child onwards, in line with the Maternity Benefit Act.

1.2 Up to **8 weeks** of the leave may be taken before the expected date of delivery.

1.3 Maternity leave is paid at **full salary**, including fixed allowances. Variable pay for the period is calculated per the Compensation Policy.

1.4 Application must be made at least **8 weeks** before the intended start date, with a medical certificate stating the expected date of delivery. Applications are made by email to people-ops@nimbusdyn.example and are processed manually; **maternity leave cannot be applied through the HR Portal leave module or the HR Assistant**.

1.5 Maternity leave is **not deducted** from Casual, Sick or Privilege Leave balances. Because of a limitation in the legacy payroll system, the request is recorded in the portal under the Privilege Leave bucket with a note; this does not reduce the employee's PL balance.

1.6 **During maternity leave, no other category of leave can be applied.** Any leave request from an employee whose status is `on_leave` for maternity is rejected. Casual and Sick Leave for the year continue to be credited and remain available after the employee returns.

1.7 Privilege Leave **continues to accrue** during maternity leave.

1.8 Employees may request an additional **up to 4 weeks of unpaid leave** immediately after maternity leave, and may request WFH for up to 8 weeks after returning, subject to the role permitting it.

1.9 Maternity leave for a miscarriage or medical termination is 6 weeks of paid leave immediately following the event, on production of a medical certificate.

## 2. Paternity leave

2.1 Male employees are entitled to **10 working days** of paid paternity leave per child, for up to two children.

2.2 Paternity leave must be taken **within 6 months** of the birth. It may be split into a maximum of **two blocks**.

2.3 Paternity leave is applied by email to people-ops@nimbusdyn.example with the birth certificate or hospital discharge summary. It is administered manually and is **not available through the HR Assistant**.

2.4 Paternity leave is not deducted from any other balance.

## 3. Adoption and surrogacy

3.1 An employee who is the primary caregiver of an adopted child below 3 months of age, or a commissioning mother, is entitled to **12 weeks** of paid adoption leave from the date the child is handed over.

3.2 An employee who is the secondary caregiver is entitled to 10 working days, on the same terms as paternity leave.

## 4. Return to work

4.1 Employees returning from maternity or adoption leave may opt for a **phased return** of reduced hours (6 hours per day at full pay) for the first 4 weeks.

4.2 Nimbus Dynamics provides a **crèche facility** at the Hyderabad and Bengaluru offices, and a crèche reimbursement of up to ₹8,000 per month at Mumbai, for children up to 6 years of age.

4.3 Nursing breaks of two 30-minute breaks per day are available until the child is 15 months old.

## 5. Status in the HR Portal

5.1 An employee on maternity, paternity (during a block) or adoption leave has the status **`on_leave`** in the HR Portal. Leave balances remain visible but cannot be used until the status returns to `active`.
