# Nimbus Dynamics — Exit and Notice Period Policy (NDPL-HR-008)

**Document ID:** NDPL-HR-008 · **Version:** 2.2 · **Effective:** 1 January 2026 · **Owner:** People Operations

## 1. Notice period

1.1 Confirmed permanent employees must give **60 calendar days'** notice of resignation. Employees on probation must give **15 days'** notice (NDPL-HR-003).

1.2 The notice period starts on the day the resignation is accepted in the HR Portal. The employee's status changes to **`notice_period`** and the last working day is recorded in the profile.

1.3 The company may, at its discretion, waive part of the notice period. Buy-out of the notice period by the employee is permitted only with approval from the department head, at a cost of the gross salary for the unserved days.

1.4 The company may place an employee on **garden leave** for all or part of the notice period. Garden leave is paid and does not consume any leave balance.

## 2. Leave during the notice period

2.1 **No Casual Leave or Privilege Leave may be availed during the notice period.** The purpose of notice is knowledge transfer, and leave defeats that purpose. Leave requests of these types from an employee on notice are rejected by the HR Portal.

2.2 **Sick Leave** during notice is permitted only with a medical certificate for every day of absence, regardless of duration.

2.3 Compensatory Off may be availed during notice period with the reporting manager's approval, but expires as usual and is not paid out.

2.4 Any leave availed during the notice period, other than certified Sick Leave, **extends the notice period** by the same number of days.

2.5 Previously approved leave that falls within the notice period is **automatically cancelled** when the resignation is accepted, and the balance is re-credited.

## 3. Leave settlement at exit

3.1 **Privilege Leave:** The unused PL balance on the last working day is **encashed in full** in the final settlement, calculated on basic salary. There is no cap on exit encashment.

3.2 **Casual Leave and Sick Leave:** lapse on the last working day. They are not encashed.

3.3 **Compensatory Off:** forfeited on the last working day.

3.4 **Negative balance:** If the employee has taken more leave than accrued (for example, PL availed in advance), the excess days are recovered from the final settlement.

## 4. Full and final settlement

4.1 The full and final (F&F) settlement is processed within **45 days** of the last working day and includes salary for days worked, PL encashment, approved reimbursements, and deductions for notice shortfall, asset non-return or advances.

4.2 The relieving letter and experience letter are issued on the last working day, provided all assets are returned and clearance is complete.

## 5. Clearance

5.1 The employee must obtain clearance from IT (laptop, access card, accessories), Finance (advances, corporate card), Admin (locker, parking) and the reporting manager (knowledge transfer document) before the last working day.

5.2 Access to all company systems, including the HR Portal and the HR Assistant, is revoked at the end of the last working day. Former employees with queries about their settlement should email people-ops@nimbusdyn.example.

## 6. Termination by the company

6.1 Termination for cause (misconduct under NDPL-HR-011) is without notice. Termination for other reasons follows the notice period in Section 1 or payment in lieu.

6.2 An employee whose employment has ended has the status **`inactive`** in the HR Portal. Inactive records are retained for 7 years for statutory purposes but are not accessible to the former employee.

## 7. Rehire

7.1 Former employees who left in good standing may be rehired. Prior service counts towards tenure-based benefits if the gap is less than 12 months.
