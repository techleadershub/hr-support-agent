# Nimbus Dynamics — Departmental Leave Restrictions and Blackout Periods (NDPL-HR-013)

**Document ID:** NDPL-HR-013 · **Version:** 1.1 · **Effective:** 1 January 2026 · **Owner:** People Operations, with the Finance, Sales and Engineering leadership

## 1. Purpose

Some departments have periods in the year when business operations cannot tolerate planned absence. During these **blackout periods**, Casual Leave and Privilege Leave are restricted for the department concerned. This document lists the restrictions. It supplements NDPL-HR-001 and, for the departments named, takes precedence over the notice periods in that policy.

## 2. Finance department — quarter close

2.1 The Finance department (all employees whose department is recorded as **Finance**) observes a blackout during quarter close: the **last 5 working days of each quarter and the first 3 working days of the following quarter**.

2.2 For 2026 the Finance blackout windows are:

| Quarter | Blackout window |
|---|---|
| Q1 close | 25 March – 3 April 2026 (working days only; 3 April is a holiday) |
| Q2 close | 24 June – 3 July 2026 |
| Q3 close | 24 September – 6 October 2026 (working days only; 2 October is a holiday) |
| Q4 close | 22 December – 6 January 2027 (working days only; shutdown days excluded) |

2.3 During a Finance blackout, **no Casual Leave or Privilege Leave is approved** except for a medical emergency or bereavement, which requires the Finance Controller's approval in addition to the reporting manager's. Sick Leave is permitted with a medical certificate for every day of absence.

2.4 Leave requests from Finance employees for dates inside a blackout window are **not rejected automatically by the HR Portal**; they are routed to the Finance Controller and are declined unless they meet 2.3. Employees are expected to know the blackout windows before applying. The HR Assistant should warn the employee before submitting such a request.

## 3. Sales department — month end

3.1 Employees in the **Sales** department may not take Privilege Leave on the **last 2 working days of any month**. Casual Leave on those days requires the approval of the VP Sales.

3.2 The annual sales kickoff (second week of April) and the Q4 push (1–20 December) are blackout periods for PL for all Sales employees.

## 4. Engineering — release freeze

4.1 Engineering teams observe a **release freeze in the two weeks before each major platform release**. Release dates are published by the CTO's office each quarter. During a freeze, PL requests of more than 2 days require the Engineering Manager's approval and a named backup on the on-call rota.

4.2 There is no restriction on Casual Leave or Sick Leave during a release freeze.

## 5. All departments — team coverage rule

5.1 Not more than **30% of a team** (rounded down, minimum 1 person) may be on planned leave on the same day. Managers may decline PL requests that breach this rule, even if all other conditions are met.

## 6. Exceptions

6.1 Exceptions to any restriction in this document are granted only in writing by the department head and the HRBP, and must be attached to the leave request.
