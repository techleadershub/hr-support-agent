# Nimbus Dynamics — HR Assistant: Frequently Asked Questions (NDPL-HR-FAQ)

**Document ID:** NDPL-HR-FAQ · **Version:** 1.6 · **Last updated:** 30 August 2026 · **Owner:** People Operations

> This FAQ is a convenience summary. Where it differs from a numbered policy document (NDPL-HR-001 to NDPL-HR-013), **the policy document prevails** (see NDPL-HR-001, preamble).

## About the HR Assistant

**What can the HR Assistant do?**
It can answer questions about HR policies, show your own leave balance, show the holiday calendar for your location, show your own profile, and submit a leave request on your behalf for Casual Leave, Sick Leave, Privilege Leave and Compensatory Off (and Loss of Pay for contractors).

**What can it not do?**
It cannot cancel or modify a leave request, apply for maternity, paternity, bereavement, marriage or study leave, record work from home, submit expenses, approve leave, or look at any employee's records other than yours. For those, use the HR Portal or email people-ops@nimbusdyn.example.

**Does the HR Assistant approve my leave?**
No. It submits the request. Your reporting manager approves it. Until it shows `APPROVED`, your leave is not confirmed.

**Which employee ID does the HR Assistant use?**
The one you are logged in with. It cannot switch to another employee, even if you give it the ID.

## Leave balances

**How many casual leaves can I carry forward?**
None. Casual Leave lapses on 31 December every year. (Until 2024, up to 5 days could be carried forward — that rule has been withdrawn.)

**How much Privilege Leave can I carry forward?**
Up to a total balance of 30 days. Anything above 30 on 31 December lapses unless encashed during the December window.

**How many sick leaves do I get?**
10 per year for permanent employees. Unused sick leave carries forward, up to a total of 30.

**Do I get leave in my first year?**
Yes, pro-rated: 1 day of CL and 1 day of SL for each completed month. PL accrues at 1.5 days a month but you can use it only after confirmation.

**Why does my balance show PL but the assistant says I cannot use it?**
You are probably on probation. PL accrues during probation but can be availed only after you are confirmed.

**Why does my balance show 0.5 days somewhere?**
Half days of CL or SL are recorded as 0.5. Late marks (3 in a month) also deduct 0.5 CL.

## Applying for leave

**How much notice do I need for casual leave?**
Two working days. In an emergency, inform your manager the same day and regularise within two working days of returning.

**Can I take casual leave for a week?**
No. Casual Leave is capped at 3 consecutive working days. Apply for Privilege Leave instead. CL and PL cannot be joined together in one stretch.

**How much notice do I need for privilege leave?**
7 calendar days for up to 3 days of PL; 15 calendar days for longer PL.

**Can I apply for leave on a holiday?**
No. Holidays and weekends are already non-working days, so the portal rejects such requests. Check the calendar for your location first — regional holidays differ between Hyderabad, Mumbai and Bengaluru.

**What is the sandwich rule?**
If you take CL or PL on the working day before and the working day after a weekend or holiday, the weekend/holiday days are also deducted. It does not apply to sick leave or comp off.

**I am on notice period. Can I take leave?**
Not Casual or Privilege Leave. Only certified Sick Leave, and Comp Off with your manager's approval.

**I am a contractor. What leave do I have?**
None from Nimbus Dynamics. Record days off as LOP through the portal or the assistant.

**I have run out of leave. What now?**
Additional absence is Loss of Pay, which needs your manager and your HRBP to approve. The assistant cannot apply LOP for permanent employees; contact your HRBP.

**Can I apply sick leave for yesterday?**
Yes, within 3 working days of returning to work. More than 2 consecutive days needs a medical certificate.

**When does my comp off expire?**
60 days after the day you earned it. The expiry date is shown against each comp off day in your balance.

**I applied for leave but want to change the dates.**
Cancel the existing request in the HR Portal and apply again. The assistant cannot cancel requests, and it cannot submit a second request that overlaps the first one.

## Holidays and shutdown

**Is Diwali a holiday in 2026?**
Yes, Monday 9 November 2026 is the observed Diwali holiday at all locations. Mumbai additionally observes Govardhan Puja on Tuesday 10 November.

**Is Bathukamma a holiday?**
Only for employees whose profile location is Hyderabad (Monday 21 September 2026). Mumbai and Bengaluru employees work that day.

**What happens during the year-end shutdown?**
The offices close from 28 to 31 December. Four days are deducted from your Privilege Leave automatically. If you do not have 4 PL, the shortfall is Loss of Pay.

**Can I take optional holidays through the assistant?**
No. Optional holidays are applied in the HR Portal, at least 5 working days ahead, maximum 2 per year.

## Other

**Is work from home counted as leave?**
No. WFH is a working arrangement recorded in the attendance module. Nothing is deducted from your balance.

**Can I encash leave?**
Only Privilege Leave: up to 10 days in the December window (1–15 December), keeping at least 10 days in balance. At exit, the full PL balance is encashed.

**How long does approval take?**
Managers are expected to act within 3 working days; after that the request is escalated to the skip-level manager automatically.
