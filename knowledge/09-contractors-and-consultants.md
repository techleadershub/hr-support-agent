# Nimbus Dynamics — Contractors, Consultants and Fixed-Term Engagements (NDPL-HR-009)

**Document ID:** NDPL-HR-009 · **Version:** 1.2 · **Effective:** 1 January 2026 · **Owner:** People Operations and Procurement

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy covers individuals who work for Nimbus Dynamics under a **contract for services** rather than an employment contract: independent consultants, contractors supplied by a staffing partner, and fixed-term project engagements. Their employment type in the HR Portal is **`contractor`**.

1.2 Contractors are not employees. Nothing in the employee policies (NDPL-HR-001 to 008, 010, 012) applies to contractors unless this document says so.

## 2. Working days and holidays

2.1 Contractors follow the Nimbus Dynamics working week (Monday to Friday) and observe the **national and regional holidays** and the year-end shutdown of the location they are assigned to. Holidays are non-billable days.

2.2 Contractors are not eligible for optional (restricted) holidays.

## 3. Leave

3.1 Contractors have **no paid leave entitlement** from Nimbus Dynamics. Casual Leave, Sick Leave, Privilege Leave, Comp Off, Bereavement and Marriage Leave do not apply.

3.2 A contractor who needs a day off records it as **Loss of Pay (LOP)** in the HR Portal so that the timesheet and invoice reconcile. LOP for contractors requires only the engagement manager's approval, not the HRBP's.

3.3 Contractors may apply for LOP through the HR Assistant with leave type **LOP**. The HR Assistant must not apply any other leave type for a contractor.

3.4 Absence of more than **10 working days** in a quarter must be agreed in writing with the engagement manager and may result in a revised engagement end date.

3.5 Any paid leave a contractor is entitled to (for example, from their staffing partner) is a matter between the contractor and that partner.

## 4. Working hours and location

4.1 Contractors work the hours stated in their engagement contract. Where the contract is silent, core hours in NDPL-HR-004 apply.

4.2 Work-from-home arrangements are per the contract. Contractors must use company-issued or company-approved devices and the corporate VPN when accessing company systems.

## 5. Systems access

5.1 Contractors receive HR Portal and HR Assistant access limited to: viewing their own profile, viewing the holiday calendar, and recording LOP days. Leave balance queries return only the LOP record.

5.2 Access ends automatically on the engagement end date recorded in the HR Portal.

## 6. Conversion to permanent employment

6.1 A contractor who is offered permanent employment joins as a new employee, serves probation under NDPL-HR-003, and receives leave entitlements from the date of joining as a permanent employee. Time served as a contractor does not count towards tenure.
