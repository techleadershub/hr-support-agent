# Nimbus Dynamics — Expense and Travel Reimbursement Policy (NDPL-HR-010)

**Document ID:** NDPL-HR-010 · **Version:** 3.4 · **Effective:** 1 April 2026 · **Owner:** Finance

## 1. General rules

1.1 Employees are reimbursed for reasonable expenses incurred **wholly and necessarily** for company business, when claimed with valid receipts through the Expense module of the HR Portal.

1.2 Claims must be submitted within **30 days** of the expense date. Claims older than 30 days are rejected automatically; exceptions require Finance Controller approval.

1.3 Every claim requires the reporting manager's approval. Claims above **₹25,000** additionally require the department head's approval.

1.4 Approved claims are paid with the next salary run if approved by the 20th of the month; otherwise in the following month.

1.5 Expenses are not a leave-related matter. The HR Assistant can explain this policy but **cannot submit, check or approve expense claims**.

## 2. Domestic travel

2.1 Travel must be booked through the company travel desk. Self-booked travel is reimbursed only if the travel desk could not fulfil the request and the manager approved self-booking in advance.

2.2 **Air travel** is economy class for all grades. Flights must be booked at least 7 days in advance except for customer emergencies.

2.3 **Train travel** is AC 2-tier or Chair Car. **Local travel** at the destination is reimbursed at actuals for app-based cabs; personal vehicle use is reimbursed at ₹12 per km for cars and ₹5 per km for two-wheelers.

2.4 **Hotel** limits per night (room rent only): Metro cities ₹6,000; other cities ₹4,000. Bookings through the travel desk are preferred.

2.5 **Per diem** (meals and incidentals, no receipts needed): ₹1,500 per day for metro cities, ₹1,000 per day for other cities. Per diem is halved on the day of return if the return is before 2:00 PM.

## 3. International travel

3.1 Requires approval from the department head and the Chief Operating Officer at least 21 days before departure.

3.2 Per diem is USD 75 per day (USD 100 for the USA, UK, Switzerland and Singapore). Hotel is booked by the travel desk within the limits in the international travel schedule.

3.3 Travel insurance is arranged by the travel desk for every international trip.

## 4. Other reimbursable expenses

4.1 **Mobile phone:** up to ₹1,500 per month for employees in customer-facing or on-call roles, with the bill attached.

4.2 **Broadband:** up to ₹1,000 per month for confirmed employees who work from home at least 1 day per week (see NDPL-HR-005).

4.3 **Home office allowance:** one-time ₹10,000 on confirmation for a chair, desk or monitor.

4.4 **Team events:** ₹1,500 per person per quarter, claimed by the manager.

4.5 **Client entertainment:** at actuals with prior manager approval, itemised bill required, alcohol not reimbursed.

## 5. Not reimbursable

Personal expenses, fines and penalties, expenses for family members, laundry (except on trips longer than 5 nights), and any expense without a valid receipt where a receipt is required.

## 6. Corporate card

6.1 Employees at Manager grade and above, and frequent travellers nominated by their department head, may be issued a corporate credit card. Card statements must be reconciled in the Expense module within 15 days of the statement date.
