# Nimbus Dynamics — Holiday Calendar Policy (NDPL-HR-002)

**Document ID:** NDPL-HR-002 · **Version:** 2.1 · **Effective:** 1 January 2026 · **Owner:** People Operations

## 1. Types of holidays

Nimbus Dynamics declares four kinds of non-working days each year:

1. **National holidays** — observed at all three locations (Hyderabad, Mumbai, Bengaluru).
2. **Regional holidays** — observed only at the location named in the calendar. An employee's regional holidays are determined by the **work location recorded in their employee profile**, not by where they happen to be on that day.
3. **Optional (restricted) holidays** — a list of festival days from which each employee may choose **up to 2 per calendar year**. Optional holidays must be applied for through the HR Portal at least 5 working days in advance; they are not deducted from any leave balance.
4. **Shutdown days** — the year-end shutdown from 28 to 31 December (see NDPL-HR-001, Section 11). Shutdown days are deducted from Privilege Leave.

## 3. Rules

3.1 A holiday that falls on a Saturday or Sunday is **not compensated** with another day off.

3.2 Employees do **not** need to apply for leave on a national holiday, on a regional holiday for their location, or on a shutdown day. A leave request for such a date is rejected by the HR Portal.

3.3 An employee who is **travelling to another location** on that location's regional holiday is not entitled to the holiday unless their profile location is updated by People Operations.

3.4 Employees who are required to work on a national or regional holiday earn Compensatory Off as per NDPL-HR-007.

3.5 The holiday calendar is published in the first week of December for the following year and cannot be changed mid-year except by a written communication from the Chief Operating Officer.

3.6 Optional holidays not availed by 31 December lapse. They cannot be carried forward or encashed.

## 4. Holidays and leave planning

4.1 When a holiday is adjacent to a weekend, employees who take leave on both sides are subject to the **sandwich rule** in NDPL-HR-001 Section 8. For example, taking Casual Leave on Thursday 1 October 2026 and Monday 5 October 2026 (with Gandhi Jayanti on Friday 2 October) deducts 5 days: Thursday, Friday (holiday), Saturday, Sunday and Monday.

4.2 A leave request that begins or ends adjacent to a holiday but does not bridge it is deducted only for the working days applied.

4.3 The HR Assistant and HR Portal both use the employee's profile location to determine which regional holidays apply.
