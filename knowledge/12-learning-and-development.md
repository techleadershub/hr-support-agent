# Nimbus Dynamics — Learning and Development Policy (NDPL-HR-012)

**Document ID:** NDPL-HR-012 · **Version:** 1.3 · **Effective:** 1 January 2026 · **Owner:** People Operations

## 1. Learning reimbursement

1.1 Confirmed permanent employees may claim up to **₹40,000 per calendar year** for courses, certifications, conferences and books relevant to their current role or an agreed career path.

1.2 Claims require pre-approval from the reporting manager in the HR Portal before enrolment, and proof of completion (certificate or attendance) within 60 days of the course end date.

1.3 Certification exam fees are reimbursed only on passing. One re-attempt per certification is reimbursed.

1.4 Employees who resign within **12 months** of a reimbursement above ₹20,000 repay the amount on a pro-rata basis from their final settlement.

1.5 Employees on probation, contractors and interns are not eligible.

## 2. Study leave

2.1 Employees may take up to **3 working days per year** of paid study leave for examinations related to an approved certification. Study leave is applied by email to people-ops@nimbusdyn.example with the exam admit card and is administered manually. It is **not** a leave type in the HR Portal and cannot be applied through the HR Assistant.

2.2 Study leave is not deducted from any other balance and does not carry forward.

## 3. Internal learning

3.1 Every employee is expected to complete a minimum of **24 hours** of learning per year, of which at least 8 hours must be from the internal Nimbus Academy catalogue. Progress is reviewed in the mid-year and annual performance conversations.

3.2 Mandatory compliance training (Code of Conduct, POSH awareness, Information Security) must be completed within 30 days of joining and annually thereafter. Non-completion blocks the annual increment until completed.

## 4. Conferences

4.1 Attendance at an external conference on a working day is recorded as **"On Duty"** in the attendance module, not as leave. Travel costs follow NDPL-HR-010.

4.2 Speaking at a conference on behalf of Nimbus Dynamics requires approval from the Marketing team for content and from the reporting manager for time.

## 5. Mentoring and internal mobility

5.1 Employees with 12 months of service may apply for internal openings. The current manager is informed after the employee clears the first interview round.

5.2 Employees with 18 months of service may enrol as mentors in the Nimbus Mentoring programme.
